## North star

*Why the promise in §1 is worth keeping. §1 states what Co does for a member; this section states what it is for. Where the two seem to disagree, §1 governs delivery and the disagreement is recorded in §0.*

**The sentence said in rooms** (locked 2026-08-29): *Co is what happens when someone who knows you completely spends every second working on your life. It does what you ask, but a lot more that you never thought to.* That is positioning. No gate in this document depends on "completely," and §0 is explicit that it is not an operating requirement. The operating promise is §1.

**Why it has to exist.** What changes a life is usually an encounter nobody arranged: a passage, a person, an opening. Whether it reaches you depends on someone who knows you well enough to recognize it and who also stands far enough outside your world to have seen it. Those two do not co-occur in one human. Co is the first thing that can be both. That is the whole reason §1 says "what you would have missed" and §8-A is about an opening rather than a task.

**Why now.** Background agents will be near-free and everywhere within a year. What stays scarce is a record of one person deep enough to know what to point them at, and that record can only be given, never taken. The deposit is the giving. That is why §3 protects it above every other behavior in this file.

**Why restraint is law, not taste.** Frame's own usage analysis (`../../frame/claims/proactive_tick.py`, retirement note dated 2026-08-22): 147 of 150 outbound messages answered no question, and the founder's weekly deposits fell from 68 to 2. Unrequested interpretation destroyed the input it depended on. §7 and §15 exist because of that number.

**The silence corollary.** The objective is a life made more chosen, not a relationship made more active. Co can work continuously backstage while saying nothing for days. A day with zero messages can be excellent product behavior; model curiosity, novelty, available content, engagement, and the desire to demonstrate work never create a reason to interrupt.

**What a member asked for, verbatim,** the first time one asked unprompted (2026-09-01): *"I just wanna say xxx and it knows."* Not a briefing and not a coach. A place to put a thing where it lands. §1's entry promise is that sentence made operational.

**The limits that make it credible.** Co does not decide what you want, does not speak in your name, and does not walk into a room for you. §5 is those three as a table.

**Banked, do not re-litigate:** the line above; consumer first, not B2B; the deposit as the entrance; proven, cheap-to-verify work before novel work. A disagreement with this section is recorded in §0, not held as a competing definition of success.

## 1. The objective

> Co is a private counterpart that knows your life and the world at once. It tells you what you would have missed, and does the small things that follow—so more of your life is chosen instead of defaulted.

The focused entry promise is:

> Send Co what catches your attention. Find it again, see connections you missed, and get help doing something with it.

The deeper outcome is that things a person cares about stop disappearing between thinking about them and living them. Better understanding, enjoyment, connection, rest, decisions, and practical relief all count. Not everything should become a task.

The member does not need to learn prompting, select tools, supervise specialists, journal daily, or commission every useful action. Co earns initiative through demonstrated judgment and scoped permission. It never earns permission merely by sounding insightful.

### The first audience and expansion hypothesis

Start with people who already send themselves links, screenshots, and thoughts, and have something they are exploring. Recruit a small community of curious, creative people, including non-founders and people who do not enjoy operating AI.

This is a behavioral entry point, not a universal claim about what everyone wants. Test whether saving is an easier entrance than task delegation and whether Co's additional intelligence improves on ordinary capture/retrieval.

Expand into other life situations only after repeatable value and supportable economics are demonstrated. A general architecture does not require a general-purpose launch.

## 2. Work backward from ten stars

### Ten-star experience

Across an ordinary month the member:
- Deposits a fragment, voice note, image, article, or video without inventing a question.
- Trusts that the original survives and can be found by approximate recollection.
- Receives a passage, connection, person, or opportunity that genuinely changes what they see.
- Sees several independent ideas connected without being handed a diagnosis or fixed identity.
- Finds useful groundwork already done, and permitted work completed while they were elsewhere.
- Can disagree once and see Co accommodate the correction.
- Can leave for a week without guilt, streak loss, or an invented emergency.
- Can ask what Co has been doing and receive a truthful account of work, uncertainty, limits, and outcomes.
- Retains control over their life, relationships, data, and authority.

The feeling is dependable care and competence, not a machine pretending to be constantly busy.

### The ladder backward

| Stage | What must be true |
|---|---|
| Ten stars: durable personal value | Judgment improves with familiarity; benefits extend outside the software; reduced dependence can be success. |
| Compelling product | People voluntarily return, pay, and identify concrete value; attention and correction burdens stay acceptable. |
| Focused pilot | A new member can deposit, retrieve, receive a worthwhile contribution, and use prepared or completed work through one coherent relationship. |
| Internal release candidate | The complete loop survives failures, respects authority, and passes credible behavioral comparisons. |
| Current foundation | Durable execution and many component mechanisms exist; several semantic, attention, live-integration, and product gates remain open. |

No stage is earned by counting implemented features.

## 3. The interaction contract: a place to put things, with an agent behind it

### Deposits are not commands

Distinguish direct requests, thought deposits, conversation, corrections, permissions, and explicit commitments. Ambiguity is allowed; storing a fragment does not require assigning it a life meaning.

- Preserve original text/media references, source identity, event time, import time, and coverage.
- Preserve a voice note alongside its transcript when retention consent permits; transcript errors must be correctable.
- Sharing establishes attention, not agreement, aspiration, intent, identity, or permission.
- Most deposits need no substantive answer. A tapback or short acknowledgment may suffice.
- If Co acknowledges that something was saved, durable acceptance must already exist.
- Inaccessible, partial, deleted, or unsupported sources remain honestly labeled. Never imply watching a full video from metadata or snippets.
- Extraction and interpretation run asynchronously. Failure must not silently lose the original or block ordinary conversation.
- Do not require an explanation of why each item mattered.
- Do not turn optional questions into an accumulating homework queue.

### Conversation remains first class

Direct questions deserve answers. Warmth, disagreement, humor, and intellectual exploration remain available. No universal no-question rule, forced hedging prefix, or sentence limit should substitute for conversational judgment.

Reflection should usually come through a concrete connection, example, source, contrast, or timely observation. A member can also explicitly ask for deeper interpretation. Hypotheses remain uncertain, inspectable, and correctable; repeated topic overlap is insufficient evidence of a hidden motive.

Keep originals, derived interpretations, explicit goals, and granted authority separate. Do not treat the member's most intense historical work period as their permanent standard.

### One relationship, several useful destinations

iMessage is the first entrance and notification surface. Work may live in an authorized document, calendar, draft, or existing workflow. The member should not have to open a chat to benefit.

A later finite surface may expose originals/search, worthwhile returns, prepared work, approvals, and activity history. Do not build an infinite feed or mandatory dashboard to compensate for weak usefulness.

Direct replies, proactive contributions, approvals, cancellations, and receipts share durable conversational state. CLI interaction remains supported for testing, operators, and power users; consumer adoption must not depend on it.

## 4. What "working on your behalf 24/7" means

It means Co holds responsibility between interactions. It does not mean an always-running model, indiscriminate surveillance, unrestricted spending, or messages proving that it is awake.

The primary loop is:

```text
deposit / authorized source change / scheduled condition / explicit request
  -> durable acceptance and provenance
  -> relevance triage and existing-matter lookup
  -> retrieve / investigate / prepare / monitor / request authority / act / stay quiet
  -> verify outcome or preserve uncertainty
  -> arbitrate whether and when anything should reach the member
  -> learn from evidence and corrections
```

### Three work lanes

1. **Respond:** satisfy the current conversation using readily available context; do not wait for speculative background analysis.
2. **Investigate and prepare:** explore promising connections, read sources, compare options, create useful artifacts, and resolve bounded unknowns.
3. **Maintain and fulfill:** watch explicit conditions, honor promises, execute authorized work, reconcile uncertain effects, and detect broken dependencies.

Foreground work, time-sensitive obligations, and repairs must not starve behind speculative research. Unbounded foreground traffic must not starve accepted obligations either. Admission, scheduling, concurrency, and budgets need separate controls.

### Durable responsibilities

Reuse and extend existing concepts:
- **Matter:** an evolving situation with provenance, relevance, last review, and a reason to retain or retire it.
- **Inquiry:** a bounded uncertainty whose resolution could improve a decision, contribution, or action.
- **Watch:** a defined condition, authorized source, freshness requirement, wake policy, expiration, and retirement rule.
- **Promise:** an explicit responsibility Co accepted, with deadline or next check and an honest outcome.
- **Mandate:** standing authority with exact scope, limits, expiration, and revocation.
- **Work order:** a bounded deliverable with predeclared postconditions and authority.
- **Opportunity:** a candidate useful opening with evidence, validity window, uncertainty, and possible consequence.

A deposit may update a matter or motivate a cheap read. It does not automatically become a goal, an endless watch, or a promise. Internal relevance hypotheses cannot authorize external actions.

Each durable unit needs tenant ownership, initiating cause, status, next wake/deadline, release version, budget, authority reference, evidence links, cancellation semantics, and terminal reason. Maintain one authoritative owner of responsibility, even when specialists collaborate.

### Waking without waste

Prefer signed events and change notifications where supported; use bounded polling otherwise. Design explicit catch-up after missed events, deduplicate arrivals, and periodically verify source subscriptions/cursors.

Wake for a relevant change, a due responsibility, a deadline, a completed dependency, or a bounded re-evaluation—not simply to generate a daily observation.

Freshness is source- and purpose-specific:
- Track last successful sync, cursor/watermark, coverage, last observed change, and failure separately.
- An old newest message can mean a quiet inbox, not failed ingestion.
- Fresh email does not establish a fresh calendar or current location.
- Event time, ingestion time, and user-confirmed current context must not be conflated.
- Missing permission or stale required context prevents the dependent claim/action; unaffected services may continue.
- Materially degraded accepted services require an honest, proportionate notice or repair path.

### Quiet usefulness and visible accountability

Safe preparation may happen without notification. Evidence of work should be available on demand, not broadcast after every internal operation.

Notify when an answer was requested, a consequential approval is needed, delay loses meaningful value, a promised result is due, an uncertain effect needs resolution, or an important service is broken. Routine completed work follows the member's receipt preference, subject to safety obligations.

Every optional foreground message must pass a member-delta test at delivery time: if removing the message would not materially change what the member can understand, choose, avoid, or get done in the relevant window, do not send it. "Interesting," "personalized," and "technically new" are not sufficient. Prefer doing safe groundwork, placing a useful artifact where it belongs, folding a thought into a natural live conversation, or retaining it for retrieval over initiating a new interruption.

No fake activity, simulated effort, or "I've been thinking about you" theater. Explain what actually ran, why it mattered, and what remains uncertain.

## 5. Autonomy without permission confusion

Initiative decides what is worth exploring. Authorization decides what may happen.

| Activity | Default boundary |
|---|---|
| Preserve a deliberate deposit | Within disclosed storage/retention and processing consent. |
| Read connected private sources | Only within a current source-specific grant and permitted purpose. |
| Research public material | Within configured research permission, privacy limits, and spend budget; minimize private context sent to search/providers. |
| Prepare a private artifact | Within an authorized workspace and bounded local-write scope; no silent overwrite or external sharing. |
| Add a calendar hold or alter an external record | Exact approval or a narrow standing mandate; "reversible" alone is not permission. |
| Send, book, buy, publish, contact, or operate a business | Consequence-specific approval or explicit bounded mandate, with outcome verification. |
| Change prompts, skills, models, or policy | Proposed and evaluated release change; never an agent's unilateral authority expansion. |

Mandates specify allowed tools/resources, recipients, amounts, frequency, duration, data disclosure, reporting, and revocation. Batch approvals may be useful only when the actual scope is clear. No blanket "act for me" interpretation.

Recheck authority and lease ownership immediately before effect initiation. Revocation fences new effects and queued successors; in-flight remote effects require reconciliation, not fictitious cancellation. Provider idempotency or reconciliation is required where duplicate consequences are possible.

No third-party outreach or matching across private member data without appropriate explicit consent. No secret exposure to model context, screenshots, logs, traces, or exports.

A larger business-agent capability is a later extension of verified responsibilities, not a pilot requirement.

## 6. Investigation: adopt Substrate's methods, not its assumptions

Build one bounded inquiry path inside the existing Co runtime before introducing another service or scheduler.

### Inquiry contract

An inquiry records:
- The question and concrete benefit of resolving it.
- Trigger and evidence available at the time.
- Competing hypotheses and what would falsify them.
- Whether it is answerable from records, public research, the member, or currently unavailable evidence.
- Planned retrieval, privacy boundary, cost/time/step budget, and stop condition.
- Findings, coverage, counterevidence, remaining uncertainty, and next permitted consequence.

Investigate with progressive query reformulation and targeted retrieval; a counterevidence pass must be able to retrieve evidence absent from the initial bundle. Merely renaming a reread an independent search does not qualify.

Use deterministic computations for well-defined counts and dates. SQL correctness does not make a construct valid: accepted invitation is not attendance; read/archive state is not reliable proof of attention; a subscription is not enjoyment. State assumptions and missing denominators.

Models can propose questions, make semantic connections, evaluate analogies, and interpret evidence. Do not prohibit the inference required for discovery; prohibit presenting it as established fact.

Human-answerable does not mean ask immediately. Hold, discard, or raise one natural question when its benefit exceeds the burden. A wrong interpretation must be directly correctable; never insist that the member reveal it through behavior instead.

Inquiry creation grants no delivery authority. Investigate privately first. Co may ask the member only when the answer could unlock a concrete present benefit, permitted records or public research cannot resolve it, one concise question is likely to collapse material uncertainty, the timing or current conversational opening is appropriate, and no recent equivalent question or correction makes it repetitive. Otherwise the inquiry stays backstage, expires, or is retired. Do not initiate conversation to harvest biography, solicit reflection, prove attentiveness, or keep the relationship warm.

### Temporal working understanding

Extend existing evidence and personal-model projections instead of constructing a second personality database. Maintain original observations, current working beliefs, explicit wants, source affinities, and unresolved matters as different objects.

- Combine lexical retrieval, semantic matching, entity resolution, and dated context.
- Distinguish quotation, negation, paraphrase, contradiction, supersession, and recurrence.
- Select historical evidence by relevance and temporal diversity, not just the final two messages of each month.
- Keep recent change visible without allowing it to erase a longer record.
- Track retrieval uncertainty; an empty or failed search does not prove novelty.
- Learn source affinity without making it an endorsement assumption or an echo-chamber boundary.
- Preserve the option of outside sources that productively challenge the member's usual references.
- Cite provenance when useful or requested; never make a research report the compulsory surface of a good observation.

Preserve all originals and rejected hypotheses subject to member retention/deletion choices. Derived projections must be rebuildable and must not turn model output into independent evidence.

### Substrate adoption boundary

Adopt the question/evidence/unknown split, progressive retrieval, claim-licensing distinctions, temporal validation, and ablation discipline. Adapt them to tenant-neutral contracts and synthetic fixtures.

Do not transplant founder constants, biography prompts, identity archetypes, universal calendar-based relationship rankings, or private-data network effects. Review the calendar acceptance/attendance conflation before reuse. Do not treat one mailbox's prediction result as evidence about every person's life.

## 7. Attention is a scarce resource, not a message quota to fill

Maintain candidate states: proposed, investigating, prepared, held, suppressed, expired, delivered, failed, or otherwise terminal with a reason. Account for missed opportunities as well as emitted messages.

Before surfacing anything, ask:
- What useful delta does this add beyond what the member already knows?
- What evidence supports that delta, and what might make it wrong?
- Why now? What happens if it waits or never surfaces?
- Would quiet preparation be more useful?
- Does another pending contribution subsume this one?
- What is happening in the live conversation?
- What interruption, social pressure, or correction burden might this create?
- Would deleting this message leave the member's understanding, choices, and outcomes unchanged? If so, suppress it.

Use semantic knownness checks selectively after cheap deduplication. Do not add a serial model call to every deposit. Evidence search failures yield uncertainty, not novelty.

One relationship-level arbiter coordinates direct conversation, media replies, proactive findings, approvals, promise receipts, and safety notices. Recheck latest context, validity, authority, suppression, and delivery budget immediately before dispatch. Serialize competing admissions appropriately.

Count actual delivery/admission reservations and delivery times, not the original creation time of aged held work. Release unused reservations safely. Define exceptions for requested replies and safety notices explicitly; report them separately so they cannot hide excessive unsolicited traffic.

Every hold has a reconsideration trigger or expiration. Reconsideration may revise, merge, suppress, or discard content. No stale insight is sent simply because quiet hours ended.

Positive feedback does not expand authority or attention limits. Negative feedback must distinguish wrong content, timing, repetition, and lack of relevance where evidence permits. An exact-topic ban is a guardrail, not the full learning strategy.

Questions and optional messages spend relationship trust, not a quota that refreshes into an obligation to speak. Keep a durable reason for every initiate/hold/suppress/expire decision. Silence must result from judgment, not a disabled worker; interruption must result from member benefit, not an available generation.

## 8. Complete useful work, not just suggestions

Bridge accepted intelligence to the existing work-order engine:
1. Specify the useful deliverable or outcome.
2. Determine required authority and dependencies.
3. Prepare what can safely be prepared now.
4. Request only missing material authority or information.
5. Execute within scope.
6. Verify the named postcondition.
7. Report according to the relationship/receipt policy.
8. Preserve failures, uncertainty, corrections, and next responsibility.

"Prepared," "awaiting approval," "submitted," "accepted," "confirmed," "failed," and "uncertain" are different states. A work order cannot complete from an unrelated clock/search receipt or successful process invocation with a failed semantic result.

Build two end-to-end scenarios with the same engine:

### A. Intellectual: deposits become a useful opening
Separated thoughts or shared artifacts support a connection. Co investigates an alternative, finds a relevant outside framework or opportunity, and produces something usable: a source passage with context, a decision brief, a concrete comparison, or an authorized next step.

Success can be clarified understanding; no action is compulsory. The source must exist, fit the actual evidence, and add more than flattery or restatement.

### B. Practical: a closing window meets a convenient moment
An authorized email reveals a return window. Co verifies the relevant terms and current status, uses permitted calendar/location context without inventing travel, prepares the logistics, and offers or executes a narrowly authorized step.

No financial loss estimate, claimed attendance, exact location, completed return, booking, or third-party contact may be invented. Verification distinguishes prepared instructions from an actual completed action.

Scenario B proves dependable agency; scenario A proves more than an errands assistant. Neither needs to succeed for every member or every deposit.

## 9. Portable architecture and monitored collaboration

Keep the existing runtime as the authority for durable work, effect gates, and receipts. Reuse the conceptual responsibilities of SignalRefinery, EvidenceStore, PerceptionLab, WorldScout, AttentionPolicy, WorkOffice, RelationshipComposer, and OutcomeLearner. These are boundaries to clarify, not eight mandatory deployable services.

Fit the inquiry loop into PerceptionLab/WorldScout/WorkOffice before creating new infrastructure. Adapters translate Substrate-derived methods into owned schemas. No dual canonical memory or competing autonomous scheduler.

### Foundation contract: one platform, personally specific behavior

Build a modular monolith with independently runnable workers first. Keep deployment simple while making ownership and dependencies explicit. Horizontal scaling must not require rewriting product behavior; distributing every component before measuring a bottleneck is not the objective.

| Layer | Owns | Must not own |
|---|---|---|
| Transport and source adapters | Authentication, normalization, delivery/status mapping, source cursors | Member identity, personal memory, task authority, product judgment |
| Durable runtime | Admission, scheduling, leases, retries, budgets, approvals, effect receipts | Founder-specific rules or implicit permission |
| Co product layer | Matters, inquiries, relevance, source affinity, attention, composition | Direct connector sends or bypasses around execution policy |
| Model and execution adapters | Declared capabilities, bounded invocations, typed outcomes | Canonical history or self-issued authority |
| Member surfaces | Capture, inspection, correction, approval, conversation | A separate memory, scheduler, or competing action state |

These boundaries require versioned contracts and conformance tests, not directory names alone:
- Authenticate identity below model/tool input. A phone number is a verified transport identifier, not the permanent tenant primary key; carrier changes and phone reassignment must not transfer someone's history.
- Pass tenant, authority, causation, release, deadline, and budget context through every work boundary. Reject missing or inconsistent ownership before data access or effects.
- Keep persistence behind narrowly scoped, transactional interfaces. Do not copy unrestricted SQL access into each imported organ or conceal required atomicity behind a generic CRUD wrapper.
- Commit state transitions and durable successor/outbox records atomically. Transport acknowledgment, queue delivery, and remote side effects are separate failure boundaries with idempotent replay and explicit reconciliation.
- Declare schema and payload compatibility for delayed jobs, retained agents, rolling upgrades, and rollback. Preserve supported old jobs or quarantine them visibly; never reinterpret them silently.
- Run shared adapter contract tests against each supported backend/provider. Storage portability must preserve fencing, uniqueness, transaction, and ordering semantics, not merely matching method names.
- Keep application instances free of untracked local authoritative state. A single-node SQLite cell is an explicit supported topology; multiple independent cells must not pretend to share one writable database or one tenant's responsibility.
- Retain stable event/resource identities across export, restore, reindex, and backend migration. The member's existing history and corrections remain usable after a model, carrier, or worker change.

### Extraction rather than whole-system merging

Frame contributes return, watch, research, session-harvest, and work-visibility patterns. Substrate contributes bounded inquiry, evidence interpretation, and evaluation methods. Neither contributes a second cron tree, private filesystem layout, implicit founder tenant, global mutable state, or independent authority system.

For each adopted component: identify its actual callers and side effects; define its input/output and failure contract; replace personal/global dependencies; add synthetic multi-tenant and adversarial fixtures; run through Co's durable engine; compare behavior before promotion. Keep the old system unchanged until an explicitly authorized cutover.

A migration has one authoritative executor per responsibility. Shadow evaluation must be unable to send, spend, or modify external records. Replay/backfill must not replay historical effects. Cutover and rollback preserve cursors, active promises, approvals, and in-flight reconciliation; do not run two live senders to test compatibility.

Specialists may research, inspect, draft, and verify:
- Each has a bounded assignment, tools, authority ceiling, context access, budget, depth, and cancellation owner.
- Messages between agents retain provenance and cannot become member instructions.
- Shared evidence is canonical; specialist summaries are claims about it.
- Parent completion requires declared child outcomes, not successful admission.
- Monitor queued/running/waiting/blocked/completed/failed states, heartbeats, next wake, receipts, costs, and authority without exposing secrets.
- Cancellation, retry, provider failure, restart, and retained-state recovery must work across the whole tree.
- Only the coordinated relationship delivery path may contact the member.

Provider/model/reasoning/token options remain configurable per role. Preserve provider-native capabilities behind explicit capability checks, not a lowest-common-denominator illusion. Subscription-backed CLI auth is optional operator infrastructure, not assumed to license a multi-user consumer service. Production credential and commercial arrangements require separate verification.

Self-improvement remains observe -> propose -> evaluate -> approve -> version -> canary -> retain/rollback. No automatic promotion from self-grades or raw engagement, and no self-issued permissions.

## 10. Scaling constraints before scale infrastructure

### Latency and resource envelope

Foreground processing persists the deposit and uses a small current context; ingestion, transcription, memory extraction, retrieval expansion, and speculative research must not block it.

Proposed pilot engineering targets, to validate rather than market:
- p95 server-side durable inbound acceptance under 1 second on the declared pilot load.
- p95 handoff of an acknowledgment requiring no model inference under 2 seconds, when acknowledgment is appropriate.
- Measure direct-answer latency separately by route; report connector delivery delay separately from server latency.
- A large attachment, hung enrichment job, or provider outage must not freeze unrelated conversation.
- Declare provider notification/polling delay and processing delay separately for each source; do not promise universal instant awareness.

Before pilot rollout, record workload, concurrency, response deadlines, tenant daily budgets, intervention limits, and observed results. Budgets must cover cumulative background work, retries, fan-out, and tool costs, not only individual model requests.

Apply fair admission, backpressure, bounded queues, coalescing, and priority lanes. Subscription CLI usage remains token/resource accounting rather than invented dollar accounting.

### Operational evidence for a solid foundation

Before calling a deployment production-ready, publish measured limits for registered and concurrently active tenants, ingestion bursts, scheduled watches, worker concurrency, stored history, provider quotas, and retained-agent workload. Report p50/p95/p99 latency, queue age, deadline misses, error/uncertainty rates, recovery time, cost, and operator intervention. Population size alone is not a workload specification.

Release tests must include a noisy tenant alongside quiet tenants, large imports alongside live replies, a sustained provider outage, duplicate/delayed source events, an offline worker, expired credentials, a failed upgrade, and a restore into a fresh environment. Verify fairness and accepted obligations under pressure, not just aggregate throughput. Document backup recovery-point and recovery-time objectives and validate them with restore drills.

Telemetry is content-minimized and tenant-scoped. Alert on broken promises, stuck work, growing queue age, failed source renewal, exhausted retries, anomalous spend, and uncertain effects. Provide per-source/provider/tenant pause controls that preserve accepted work and audit state; no global outage should be required to stop one malfunctioning component.

The production path must be reproducibly deployable without a founder's laptop, personal directory structure, manually edited tenant rows, or consumer subscription session. Development conveniences may remain explicitly separate. Supply migrations, startup validation, health checks, secrets references, deployment/rollback instructions, and incident runbooks for the supported topology.

### Isolation, retention, and portability

Partition evidence, retrieval, secrets, agents, budgets, queues, caches, and traces by tenant. Reuse public-source enrichment only when access rights and terms permit; never let private annotations enter a shared cache.

Imports are previewed, consented, resumable, and idempotent. Preserve source logs; retain original event times. Corrections append supersession. Export/restore and deletion must be verified against exact tenant scope; preservation is not permission to prevent deletion.

No migration to Postgres, distributed queues, graph databases, or blobs merely to look scalable. Add an adapter when measured limits require it, with migration/recovery tests and no loss of identity, source history, or authority. State the supported deployment envelope honestly.

No advertising or paid-placement incentives in personal recommendations. Membership pricing is a business hypothesis to validate alongside cost and willingness to pay, not a reason to induce dependence.

## 11. Measurement: value, use, and attention are different

Keep a dashboard of measures, not one prematurely optimized score:

| Dimension | Measure | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Voluntary use | Deposit days, retrievals, requests, corrections, approvals, continued unprompted use | Raw volume is not benefit. |
| Reliable custody | Accepted deposits preserved, retrieval success, accessible originals, capture errors | Stored bytes are not understanding. |
| Useful outcomes | Verified tasks, used artifacts, explicit useful discoveries, calibrated review of contributions | A generated artifact alone is not use; praise alone is not life improvement. |
| Attention cost | Unsolicited deliveries, approvals, corrections, unwanted follow-up, management effort | Low message count alone is not good judgment. |
| Context contribution | Matched-runtime history ablation and component ablation | A tool-enabled agent beating a tool-less prompt does not prove memory value. |
| Continuing value | Retention, willingness to pay, optional interviews, concrete benefits outside Co | Inactivity alone proves neither failure nor successful independence. |
| Economics | Cost per active/retained paying tenant, useful outcome, and incident; support effort | Cheap messages do not imply affordable service. |

Maintain evidence labels: system-verified outcome, explicit member report, independently reviewed interpretation, inferred signal, or unknown. A quotation validates provenance, not the sentiment or causal claim assigned to it.

Track reflection through occasional voluntary feedback and opt-in interviews. Do not convert every insight into a rating prompt. No read receipt, archive flag, silence, or ordinary politeness becomes automatic approval.

Count all outgoing communication, with separately defined transactional, requested, substantive unsolicited, and safety categories. Text length is not a valid category classifier by itself. Report uncertain classification.

For optional foreground communication, measure avoidable-interruption rate, repeat-question rate, correction burden, and the share of candidates safely prepared, held, merged, expired, or suppressed. Review both sent candidates and legitimate silences against the same counterfactual: did this decision improve the member's situation compared with the best quiet alternative? Neither high output nor blanket silence can pass.

Explicit-positive/all-response ratios are lower bounds on reported positivity, not calibrated usefulness estimates. Estimate quality on preregistered representative samples with denominators, coverage, disagreement, and uncertainty. Preserve all-emission accounting to prevent cherry-picking.

Do not optimize daily chat usage, streaks, emotional dependence, number of insights, growth compliance, number of agents, or apparent intimacy. Retention remains commercially important; user autonomy remains a product constraint.

### Reliability and authority

- Same model, harness, tools, budgets, environment, and task with longitudinal history enabled versus withheld.

- Snapshot state and evidence as of each checkpoint; do not allow later labels, summaries, or enriched records to leak future knowledge.
- Compare with capture/retrieval alone, current Co, and a pinned relevant external harness where comparable. State capability differences rather than attributing them to memory.
- Use blinded graders calibrated against human judgments, multiple runs/noise estimates, and semantic outcome receipts for action tasks.
- Remove one belief/component at a time and include unchanged reruns. Keep mechanisms only when they earn their cost without degrading safety.
- Separate deterministic synthetic CI from explicitly authorized private/live evaluations. Unavailable private evidence is an open gate, never a silent passing skip.
- Do not block useful internal regression tests on an unavailable external baseline; do block unsupported comparative claims.

## 15. Not in the critical path

- A new harness rewrite, graph database, or eight-service deployment.
- A feed, personality dashboard, identity quiz, mandatory journaling, daily briefing, or streak system.
- Covert motive-based pressure or automatic accountability.
- A second model call on every deposit solely to classify its importance.
- More modalities or passive watch-history collection before existing deposits become useful.
- Always-on recording or broad inbox access as prerequisites for first value.
- Cross-member private graphs, viral portraits, or third-party introductions without consent.
- Autonomous business operation, general purchasing, or arbitrary browser logins.
- Self-promoting skills or recursive self-modification based on engagement.
- Claims of zero latency, complete understanding, perfect safety, or mass-market fit from local tests.

## 16. Definition of done for the focused release

The release is ready for its declared pilot scope only when:
1. Members can deposit and retrieve originals without compulsory conversation.
2. Approved sources wake durable, bounded work without foreground dependence.
3. Both investigative and practical paths run end to end, including a legitimate silent outcome.
4. Permitted actions complete with exact evidence; missing authority produces an intelligible request.
5. Co can truthfully explain current work, fulfilled promises, failures, and uncertainty on demand.
6. Corrections and revocations affect pending and future work.
7. Attention arbitration prevents stale, repetitive, or budget-breaking deliveries.
8. Crash/retry/duplicate/hostile-input tests exercise the composed paths.
9. Matched evaluations and actual non-founder evidence are reported without self-certification.
10. History, privacy, portability, cost, and operational limits remain intact.

Ten-star outcomes and mass-market adoption remain research/product ambitions after this release. Do not mark them complete from a successful pilot.
