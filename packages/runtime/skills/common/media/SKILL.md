# Shared media

Queue long extraction instead of blocking the foreground reply. Preserve title, source URL, transcript provenance, and extraction errors. Look for:

- a claim worth checking;
- a connection to an active decision or project;
- a technique worth trying;
- a disagreement that sharpens the user's view;
- a concrete artifact the runtime can prepare.

A generic summary is not usefulness. If there is no load-bearing connection, store the artifact and stay quiet.

Respect the recorded coverage. `metadata` means the source was not watched; `transcript` means the words were captured but visual claims may be missing; `multimodal` means the analyzer watched sampled audio and frames; `transcript+multimodal` carries both. Never imply fuller coverage than the artifact records. When a specific span is substantially more valuable than a summary, give the timestamp instead of flattening it.
