# kyo — brand playbook

> Part of the [design source of truth](README.md). Visual identity and its laws.
> Direction: **Cloud Instrument** — archive intimacy + aluminum precision + cloud-blue softness.

## 1. Essence

kyo is an intelligence that truly understands who you are — a confidant whose
understanding compounds. The visual world must make the user feel **remembered, not
managed**. It is a personal archive built with the precision of a beloved object: think
Muji, Leica, the Sony TPS-L2, high-end editorial publishing — never a SaaS dashboard,
therapy app, habit tracker, or chatbot.

**Core promise:** kyo helps people understand who they are becoming, not merely what they
have captured.

**Emotional result:** clarity, safety, agency, and the strange comfort of being accurately
remembered.

Brand attributes: **Thoughtful · Connected · Timeless · Human · Intentional**

Personality spectrum (both halves are binding):

| Is | Is not |
|---|---|
| Quiet | silent or cold |
| Intelligent | dashboard-like |
| Human | sentimental |
| Premium | precious |
| Precise | sterile |
| Trustworthy | institutional |
| Tactile | nostalgic |
| Earned | performative |

## 2. The two-layer law

The single most important structural rule. kyo has two visual layers that must never be
confused:

**Layer 1 — the expressive brand world** (brand book, landing pages, campaign material,
App Store imagery, onboarding moments, shareable artifacts, selected high-emotion product
moments): full Cloud Instrument material language. Binders, brushed aluminum hardware,
layered paper, translucent vellum, pale watercolor, embossing, graphite, editorial serif,
white-on-white still-life photography.

**Layer 2 — the native product interface** (navigation, tabs, toolbars, buttons, search,
text entry, chat, menus, sheets, alerts, pickers, permissions, transitions, everyday
controls): native iOS 26 conventions and Apple controls. Liquid Glass belongs to controls
and navigation. The brand emerges through content, typography, color, imagery, pacing,
and selected signature moments — never by rebuilding the operating system.

> The brand is richer than the chrome. Restraint is what lets the unusual elements keep
> their emotional weight.

Deciding test for any surface: *is this meaning, or is this chrome?* Meaning may carry
material. Chrome stays native.

## 3. The four visual pillars

Every brand expression should contain at least two:

1. **Layered archive** — binder, cards, translucent sheets, provenance, history
2. **Clouded blue** — mist, memory, calm, atmosphere, introspection
3. **Aluminum precision** — clips, rings, rails, corners, object permanence ("the frame,
   not the room" — aluminum is central to the brand, structural in expression, but it
   frames; it never coats)
4. **Quiet typography** — substantial serif display against invisible functional type

(The earlier sources' fifth pillar — the "subtle graph" — is removed per Justin's
recorded correction: no graph in the design system, UI concepts, **or future brand
direction**. Connection is expressed in words and provenance, never as linework.)

## 4. Identity

**Wordmark.** `Kyo` in EB Garamond Medium/Semibold (to be custom-refined). Strong
architectural K; elegant, unflamboyant y descender; slightly tight optical tracking;
thicker and bookish, never a hairline fashion serif. Title case `Kyo` for the mark;
lowercase `kyo` remains the prose/product spelling.

**Monogram.** A simple K in a soft rounded square or thin circle — a small object or
stamp, not a chatbot avatar. Used for app icon, loading, small marks.

**App icon.** K monogram (EB Garamond-derived) on a clouded aluminum-blue rounded square:
frosted, layered, a barely visible watercolor/vellum diffusion — not a flat gradient.
Foreground white or Ink by background tone; soft aluminum edge or inner glow. Radius
metaphor 22. Banned motifs: chat bubbles, hearts, robots, stars, sparkles, generic AI
symbols.

## 5. Color, in brand terms

Full tables live in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). The brand grammar:

- The palette reads as **a cool stack of whites before it reads as blue** — at least three
  cool-white layers before blue enters any major composition.
- **Blue is a language, not a decoration**: mist = atmosphere and inactive memory; powder
  = soft secondary surfaces; washed slate = metadata; watercolor = the primary memory
  signal and selection; steel = decisive action.
- **Aluminum** is structure and credibility: edges, rules, separators, hardware.
- **Ink** is for words, sparing emphasis — never large dark fields.
- **Excluded, permanently:** ember/orange, warm paper/cream, oat, clay, large void-black
  fields, visible grid textures, neon, saturated accents. These pull kyo into categories
  it is not in (lifestyle journal, therapy app, generic AI, sci-fi).

## 6. Materials

| Material | Meaning | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Fine paper | human record, provenance, calm | cards, archive covers, empty states |
| Vellum / translucence | privacy, layering, partial knowledge | layered panels, transitions, profile card back |
| Watercolor | interpretation — meaning forming | insight/evening cards, covers, selected states |
| Aluminum | credibility, durability, the frame | hairlines, rails, edges, hardware imagery |
| Graphite | thought, precise small marks | annotations in brand imagery |

Rules: texture is optional everywhere it appears (clean fallback required); text sits on
clean surfaces, never on strong texture; gradients only when they originate from a
photographed/painted material — no synthetic hue ramps.

## 7. Photography & imagery

Shoot real materials: translucent paper, frosted acrylic, aluminum edges, binder rings,
water droplets. Cool, soft, directional light — never warm sunset lifestyle. Quiet,
spacious composition; small blue details carry attention. Human imagery rare,
documentary, provenance-based — no stock wellness photography. Images in-product sit as
objects: paper mats, subtle shadows, soft edges.

## 8. Voice in the interface

kyo's soul constraints (MASTER.md) are design law wherever words appear in UI — including
empty states, permission primers, errors, and the paywall:

- Specific, calm, observational, earned. Warm, not soothing. Direct, not motivational.
- "I noticed…" when there is evidence; "am I right that…?" when uncertain.
- Never: sycophancy, generic affirmation, therapy-speak, productivity language, AI-feature
  claims, receipt confirmations ("got it!"), "we miss you" in any costume, guilt, red-badge
  pressure, celebration confetti.
- Errors are matter-of-fact: what happened, what to do. Refusals happen in kyo's voice.
- Case law (Justin's call, 2026-07-24): **ALL app UI text uses normal sentence
  capitalization** — first letter of every sentence capitalized, never all-lowercase,
  never Title Case for labels. This covers everything the app renders as UI: empty
  states, primers, errors, buttons, form fields, nav titles, accessibility labels, the
  paywall — even copy written in kyo's voice ("Show me your day."). The lowercase
  texting register survives ONLY in message content on conversation surfaces (chat
  bubbles, iMessage) — there, casing belongs to whatever personality fits the moment,
  never to this rule. kyo speaks in first person singular; exclamation marks are close
  to never. Mid-sentence the product name stays lowercase `kyo`; at sentence start or
  standing alone (nav title) it capitalizes to `Kyo`.

Sample of the register (from the brand book, kept as calibration):

> "You keep returning to ideas about craft and patience. I do not think this is just
> aesthetic taste. It seems like the shape of the life you want."

## 9. Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Native controls, thoughtfully configured | Custom rebuilds of OS furniture to look branded |
| One calm focal object, editorial negative space | Dense dashboard grids, widget walls |
| Hairline rules to separate | Heavy card borders everywhere |
| Blue as meaning/selection | Blue as ambient decoration |
| Serif at moments of interpretation | Serif in tiny controls or as vibe |
| Material accents on interactive elements ([TEXTURE.md](TEXTURE.md)) | Texture under body text; metallic-coated UI; skeuomorphic buttons |
| Liquid Glass on controls/nav (system APIs) | Web-style glassmorphism, fake blur recipes, glowing edges |
| Quiet dismissals, no backlog guilt | Badges, streaks, gamified pressure |
| Muji/Leica restraint | Notion/Arc/Raycast resemblance, black-mode sci-fi |
| The name's meaning carried quietly | Japanese aesthetic cosplay |
